OUR MISSION

Building the foundation for African space technology.

2060 Aerospace is an emerging technology initiative focused on aerospace software, education, research and future engineering.

WHO WE ARE

2060 Aerospace is building from Kenya to the stars.

We are an emerging African aerospace technology initiative focused on software, space education, research and future engineering.

Our Mission

Develop practical aerospace technology and create opportunities for African innovators.

Our Vision

Build a future where African technology contributes to humanity's exploration of space.

Our Values

Innovation, learning, integrity, collaboration and long-term thinking.

WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

Technology for Africa's next generation of space missions.

Our technology roadmap begins with software and research, then moves toward increasingly advanced aerospace systems as capability, partnerships and resources grow.

01 — Aerospace Software

Mission-control, simulation and operational software for learning, testing and future aerospace applications.

02 — Telemetry & Data

Tools for collecting, displaying and analyzing simulated and future aerospace telemetry and mission data.

03 — Space Systems Research

Research into satellite, payload, sensing and other space-system technologies through responsible partnerships.

04 — Education & Innovation

Practical space-technology learning and innovation opportunities for students, developers and future engineers.

THE ROAD AHEAD

From foundation to aerospace.

Our roadmap describes the technology we aim to develop as 2060 Aerospace grows. Future phases are planned development targets, not completed systems.

01 — Foundation

Establish 2060 Aerospace, build our digital platform and grow an African technology community.

02 — Software

Develop mission-control, simulation, telemetry and aerospace data tools.

03 — Research

Develop research projects and pursue collaboration with universities and technical institutions.

04 — Hardware

Explore sensors, telemetry and experimental aerospace systems through appropriate testing and partnerships.

05 — Advanced Aerospace

Pursue increasingly advanced aerospace technologies as our team, knowledge and resources grow.
